Facilitator guide

How to run the game

Option A · Facilitator mode

  1. Print the game pack on A4 paper or card and cut out the 24 instrument cards.
  2. Shuffle all cards together. With eight participants, deal exactly three to each person.
  3. Project this game and keep control of the facilitator panel.
  4. After discussion, select Break this link. The response check is optional and does not need to be revealed.

Option B · Team Challenge

  1. Create teams and give each team the public game link. One device controls the game for that team.
  2. For a hybrid group of 16, use four teams of four: two in-person and two online participants per team.
  3. For each link, discuss the eight virtual cards and choose exactly three before time runs out.
  4. Select Test our 3 cards. The game identifies missing outcomes without revealing an answer. First team through its selected links wins.

What makes a strong response?

  • It covers immediate protection, remedy or enforcement, and prevention or worker voice.
  • It includes a Workers card and combines at least two actor groups.
  • More than one three-card combination can work.
  • Do not delay urgent protection while dialogue takes place.
  • Worker voice supports detection and sustainable solutions; workers are not responsible for violations against them.

Debrief together

  1. Which information was easiest—or hardest—to surface?
  2. Which actions had to happen immediately?
  3. What remedied harm, and what prevented recurrence?
  4. What would have been missing if one constituency were absent?
  5. Who held legal power, and who held knowledge or worker trust?

Social dialogue strengthens prevention and implementation. It does not replace protection, remedy or enforcement.
